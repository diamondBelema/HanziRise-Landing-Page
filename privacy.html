Hanzi Rise

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

Hanzi Rise does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no trackers. Your learning progress stays on your device.

What the app accesses

Hanzi Rise works fully offline. It does not require an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers. All content — characters, words, stories, and your progress — is stored locally on your device.

Text-to-speech

On Android, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce characters and words. Audio is generated on-device by your system TTS service. Hanzi Rise does not record audio or transmit speech data.

Permissions

Hanzi Rise requests no special permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage beyond its own app data.

Children's privacy

Since no personal data is collected, Hanzi Rise is safe for learners of all ages, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes its data practices, this policy will be updated before any new data collection begins.
