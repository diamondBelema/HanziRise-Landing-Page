Everything inside Hanzi Rise

One app for the whole journey — from your first tone to reading real stories. A lesson alone fades; Hanzi Rise keeps bringing back what you're about to forget.

Core

FSRS spaced repetition

An FSRS scheduler picks what to review each day, so you spend time only on the words that are slipping — not the ones you already know.

Core

Stories at your level

Short stories unlock once you know about 95% of their words, so reading always feels doable — never discouraging.

Core

Hear every word

Tap the speaker to hear any word read aloud by your phone's Chinese voice, and type pinyin with tone marks built in.

New

Grammar lessons · 227 points

Grammar is taught in the same rhythm as vocabulary — small, clear points that unlock as you progress through HSK 1–4.

New

Mini games

Quick game modes that drill recognition, meaning and tones — practice that feels like play, not homework.

New

Beginner track: sounds & tones

Zero characters required. Start from pinyin, initials and finals, and train your ear to hear the four tones.

New

Writing & stroke order

Trace characters with guided stroke order and see them come alive — muscle memory meets meaning.

Core

Works fully offline

Lessons, reviews and stories live on your device. Learn on the train, on a plane, anywhere — no signal needed.

语 Grammar path

227 grammar points, unlocked in order

No 40-page chapters. Each point is a bite: one pattern, one example, one quick check — stacked into a path from 是 to 把字句.

声 Sound first

Train your ear before your eyes

The beginner track starts with pinyin and tone drills, so 妈 and 马 stop sounding identical before you meet a single character.
